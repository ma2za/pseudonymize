# The Shift in Data Privacy

As global regulations like GDPR and CCPA tighten, businesses are struggling to balance the need for data analytics with the strict requirements of user privacy. 

**pseudonymize.io** was built precisely to bridge this gap.

## Deterministic vs. Random Anonymization

Random anonymization destroys the relational integrity of your data. If user ID `123` becomes `abc` on Monday and `xyz` on Tuesday, your analytics pipelines are broken.

Deterministic pseudonymization ensures that `123` *always* becomes `abc`. You retain the relational value of the data without ever exposing the raw PII.
