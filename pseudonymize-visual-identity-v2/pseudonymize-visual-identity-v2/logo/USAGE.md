# Logo usage

Primary: `lockup-dark.svg` on dark surfaces and `lockup-light.svg` on light surfaces.

- Clear space: at least 0.5× symbol width.
- Minimum mark size: 16 px. At 16–20 px, use `mark-dark.svg` only.
- Do not recolor the cipher cut except monochrome contexts.
- Do not rotate, stretch, add glow, put inside another shield/lock, or decorate with gradients.
- The diagonal cut is a transformation boundary, not a prohibition symbol. Pair it with the wordmark whenever that distinction could be ambiguous.
